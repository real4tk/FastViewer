FastViewer - what's new
=======================

Version numbers: major.minor.patch. New features raise the middle number, bug fixes the last one.
You can see which version you have in "..." -> Settings -> About.


1.30.0  (21 Nov 2026)
  New
  - EXPERIMENTAL, OFF BY DEFAULT: Smooth loops and speed changes, like TikTok and Reels. Turn it on in
    Settings -> Videos -> "Smooth loops and speed changes (experimental)". FastViewer then draws the video itself
    (decoded on the graphics card), timed by its own sound, and gets the first pictures of the next loop ready
    before the old loop ends. Repeat starts over without the picture stopping for a moment, and speed changes (also
    holding for 2x) no longer freeze the picture. Measured on a Medal clip: the picture used to stand still
    60-180 ms at each loop and 105-140 ms at each speed change, now 0-12 ms and mostly under 30 ms.
    Not finished yet, which is why it is off: pausing and playing can still show a grey haze for a moment, short
    loops can feel a little late, and it uses noticeably more memory. A rebuilt version comes in 1.31. With it off
    (the default) videos play exactly as in 1.29, with the fixes below.
  - Settings -> Viewing -> "Double-click for full screen": turn it off and double-clicking the picture does
    nothing (F, the full screen button and Esc work as always).
  - Playback stats: Ctrl+Shift+P shows, top left, who draws the video, its frame rate, how many screen frames and
    video pictures were shown each second, and how many were skipped or late. While it is on, every second is also
    written to playback.txt (in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FastViewer), with where any picture was skipped or late.
  Fixed
  - Videos looked washed out and greyish with "Smooth loops and speed changes" (no deep blacks): the picture FastViewer
    draws was made partly see-through, so the grey behind showed through the dark parts. Now it is fully opaque; its
    colours match a standard decoder exactly (measured: 45 dB, the same picture).
  - Skipping forward again and again at the end of a clip could jam Windows' video playback for the whole program:
    nothing played any more, other videos neither, until FastViewer was ended in Task Manager. Skipping past the end
    now goes on into the next loop (with Repeat) or stops just before the end. And should a video player ever get
    stuck again, FastViewer notices within a few seconds and makes it again by itself (or, if Windows' playback is stuck
    as a whole, starts itself again on the same video and place); it is noted in errors.txt.
  - 120 fps videos were sometimes shown with fewer pictures: showing a picture could wait for WPF to let go of it,
    which held up the next screen frames. It no longer waits; measured 111-118 of 120 pictures a second in a test
    window that itself draws irregularly.
  - A song on Repeat at 2x or above 100% could stop with "can't be played": Windows' player was started over right at
    its end. Now it rests while FastViewer plays the song's sound, as with videos.
  - Crackling in the sound (also in 1.28 and 1.29, more often in 1.30): whenever FastViewer plays the sound itself
    (other speeds, volume over 100%, Even out volume boosting a quiet file, Medal clips, and every video with Smooth loops on) and
    keeps it in step with the picture, it reads the sound a tiny bit faster or slower. Each piece of sound (every
    60 ms) then started fresh and left out a fraction of a sample: a small jump, up to 16 times a second, heard as
    crackling on loud music and tones. The reading now carries on exactly from piece to piece. Measured on a loud test
    tone: 27-113 jumps in 6 s before, none now. Volume over 100% (and Even out volume) used to squeeze every loud
    peak on its own; a proper limiter now turns loud moments down smoothly a moment ahead. A loop's seam fades over
    2.5 ms, so where the end and the start of a clip's sound don't meet it no longer clicks.
  - Playback stats (Ctrl+Shift+P) only said "Playback stats on": the stats themselves were placed off screen. They
    now show top left on the video, with Smooth loops on and off.
  - Settings in a narrow window: on some pages (Buttons, Viewing) a setting's buttons or menu were pushed past the
    right edge, out of sight. Now they move under their text when there isn't room, so every setting shows at any
    window width.

1.29.0  (18 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Hold for 2x speed, like YouTube and TikTok: hold the left mouse button on a video or song and it plays at 2x
    for as long as you hold it ("2x" shows at the top); let go and it goes back to the speed it had (also 1.5x or
    0.5x). A short click still plays/pauses as before; only a press that stays put for about 0.3 s counts, so
    dragging (a zoomed-in video, the mini player) works as always. Also in the mini player. The sound speeds up
    with it without a break, Medal clips included. On a paused video holding plays it at 2x, and it plays on.
    Letting go goes straight back to normal: no moment of slowed-down sound.
  - Faster or slower sound keeps its normal pitch, like YouTube, TikTok and Telegram: voices just talk faster
    instead of sounding like chipmunks (at every speed, 0.25x to 5x, and with hold for 2x). "Keep speed" when you
    save a clip, make a GIF or send to Discord does the same. Settings -> Videos -> "Change pitch with speed"
    brings back the old way, where faster also sounds higher.
  - Even out volume (Settings -> Videos, on): loud videos and songs play quieter and quiet ones a little louder,
    so they all sound about equally loud. Each file is measured once in the background, the way TV and streaming
    services measure loudness (EBU R128), and remembered, so it's never measured twice. The files next to the one
    you're watching are measured ahead, so switching is just as instant. A Medal clip is measured as you hear it
    (game and mic together). Your volume slider works on top of it. Quiet files get at most 6 dB louder, nearly
    silent ones and hiss not at all, and nothing is made so loud it distorts. Not measured yet (the very first
    time you open a file)? It plays as it is and slides to its level within a few seconds, without a jump.
    Send to Discord gets the same level, so your friends hear your clips at a normal loudness too.
  Changed
  - At another speed the sound and the picture now change speed together (Windows' player stands still for a
    tenth of a second whenever its speed changes; the sound is timed around that instead of being slowed down
    to wait for it), and after a speed change FastViewer keeps playing the sound itself until you pause, so
    there's no small dip when Windows' player takes it back.
  - Repeat with FastViewer's own sound (other speeds, Medal clips, after a speed change) loops seamlessly: no silence
    at the end, no bit of sound heard twice after the restart, and the sound in step from the first picture. Every bit
    of every sound track plays to the very end of the file, and the next loop's sound starts right after the last
    bit; the picture is fitted to the sound (it waits a moment before its end when the sound is a little longer).
  Fixed
  - Mini player: letting go after holding for 2x paused the video. It now always plays on at the speed it had; only
    a short click plays or pauses. Moving the mini player starts once you actually drag.
  - After a few quick holds for 2x the sound could run up to 3% slower or faster for a few seconds. Now it plays at
    its speed: tiny differences with the picture are evened out at most 0.4% (as before 1.29), and only in the
    4 seconds after a speed change at most 1.5% at the same pitch; never by leaving out or repeating bits of sound.
  Known
  - Windows' player still stands still for about a tenth of a second whenever the speed changes (also when you let
    go of a hold); FastViewer times the sound around it. Videos without sound don't have it.

1.28.0  (15 Nov 2026)
  New
  - English translation of subtitles, for learning a language. Video "..." -> Subtitles -> Translate to English
    translates the subtitles or automatic captions line by line into English, on this PC. It starts at the line
    you're watching and each line shows as soon as it's done. The original shows on top and the English under it
    (a little smaller); "Show original" and "Show English" in the same menu switch either off. The English is kept,
    so next time it's just there (also when you stop halfway: then only the rest is translated). It goes into
    "Burn in captions" and "Save a copy with captions" too, the subtitle editor has an English box for each line,
    and "Save English as a file..." writes it as an .srt. This needs a one-time download of about 1.2 GB (the
    translation model Hy-MT2 by Tencent, and llama.cpp to run it); after that it works without internet. With a
    good graphics card a whole film takes about a minute, on the processor alone about 6 minutes.
  - Faster captions (Parakeet). An optional extra download (about 760 MB) in Settings -> Videos: a newer speech
    model by NVIDIA that writes captions about 6 times faster than the large Whisper model (a whole film in about
    10 minutes instead of an hour), with better timing. It knows 25 European languages (English, Dutch, German,
    French, Spanish, Russian, Ukrainian, Polish...); FastViewer first listens to a few seconds of the video to find
    the language and uses Whisper for the others. "Caption engine" (Automatic / Parakeet / Whisper) picks it
    yourself. On a film it misses a few more lines than the large Whisper model (5.5% against 4.7% of the spoken
    lines); with music and songs the large model is still a little better.
  - "Copy error report" in Settings -> About. When something goes wrong in FastViewer, it copies a short report
    (FastViewer's and Windows' version and the last errors, without your file names or folders) that you can
    paste into a message to whoever helps you with FastViewer. Right after an error a message offers the same,
    and if FastViewer closed because of an error, it says so the next time it starts. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  - The Info pane shows the frame rate ("60 fps"; for videos whose rate changes the average, e.g. "about 95.5 fps
    (up to 120)") and the video format (H.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9...), under Dimensions.
  Changed
  - Make smoother: the new video gets the same dates as the original (10 ms earlier), so in a folder sorted by
    date it sits right next to the original instead of at the top.
  - Settings -> About lists the programs and models FastViewer uses (Credits).
  - Send to Discord: "Burn in captions" (and "Keep speed" at another speed) are on/off switches at the top of the
    menu, remembered for next time; each size ("20 MB, Discord without Nitro", "50 MB, Nitro Basic", "500 MB, Nitro")
    starts sending right away. Make GIF and Save clip show "Keep speed" the same way.
  Fixed
  - Settings -> Buttons, Keyboard and Videos and the "..." menus: Skip back and Skip forward show the same icon as
    the buttons on the video bar, with the number of seconds; Rotate shows the same arrow as its button.
  - The subtitle editor's lines were drawn in black on dark looks (hard to read); they now take the look's text
    colour like the rest of the pane. In a see-through Custom look the pane keeps enough of its colour behind the
    text to stay readable over the video.

1.27.0  (15 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Edit subtitles. Video "..." -> Subtitles -> Edit subtitles (or Ctrl+E) opens a pane on the right with every
    line. Click a line to go there; type to change its text; - and + move its start or end by 0.1 s (hold to
    repeat, Shift: 0.5 s), "Now" puts it at this moment of the video. "Shift all" moves every line at once
    (when the whole file is a bit early or late). Delete and Merge with next, "Add line here" adds a line where
    the video is, and Undo (Ctrl+Z) takes back the last change. What you change shows in the video at once.
    "Save as..." writes a new .srt; your original file is only changed when you click Save and then
    "Replace the file?". Works for subtitle files, for automatic captions and for a video without any subtitles yet.
  - A welcome screen when FastViewer starts without a file: Open a file (or drop one on the window), Settings,
    "Make FastViewer my default viewer" (only while it isn't yet; Windows asks you to pick it in Default apps)
    and the files and folders you opened last (right-click one to take it off the list; "Clear recent" empties
    it). Delete, Copy, Rotate and the other buttons for a file step out of the top bar while there's no file.
    It also shows when you delete the last file of a folder.
  Better automatic captions (measured on a whole 1 hour 45 film with the "better quality" model)
  - Films with 5.1 sound: the voices now come from the centre channel, where films put them. Windows' own mix
    buried them under music and effects, so whole stretches got no text.
  - A line stays on screen only as long as it takes to read it (1.5 s plus a little per letter, 7 s at
    most) and ends when the next one begins. Before, a line could stay for 30 seconds.
  - A block of lines written twice in a row is shown once, at the right time; "PHONE RINGING" three times in
    a row is shown once. Captions you made before are tidied up the same way, no need to make them again.
  - On that film: spoken lines without any text went from 9.8% to 4.7%, lines starting more than 3 seconds
    off from 6.9% to 1.6%, and the longest line from 30 to 6.7 seconds. Making them takes just as long.
  Changed
  - Opening Settings no longer pauses the video or song. It keeps playing (also when a setting reloads the
    folder or changes the look) until you pause it yourself.
  - The number inside the skip buttons is bigger and exactly in the middle of the circle, for every amount.
  - The rotate button and Rotate left / Rotate right in the menus have a new, clearer icon (Tabler Icons).
  - A Custom look made see-through ("See-through" lowered for the background, the bars or the pages) now also
    makes the Settings screen see-through, so the video playing behind it shows. It always stays covered enough
    to read, and the video's buttons don't show through.
  Fixed
  - Changing a colour of your Custom look closed FastViewer half a second later, once Settings > Appearance had
    been opened (since 1.23.0). Fixed, and every control of the Custom look page was tested.
  - FastViewer no longer closes when something unexpected goes wrong in its window: it writes what happened to
    errors.txt in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FastViewer and keeps running.
  - Starting FastViewer.exe on its own while FastViewer was ready in the background played the last video
    after a moment. It now shows the welcome screen and stays there (the icon by the clock still opens the
    last file).
  - Deleting the file on screen sometimes showed a random file of the folder. It now always shows the next
    file in the order you're going through (sorted, shuffled or only videos), or the one before when it was
    the last.
  - With a see-through background in a Custom look, the files FastViewer gets ready next to the one you're
    looking at (and their dark bars) could show through behind it. They never show now, whatever you choose.
  - The subtitle editor docks floating bars while it's open (the window buttons covered its own buttons),
    gets narrower with a narrow window and keeps every button usable.


1.26.0  (14 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Clips with several sound tracks (Medal: game and microphone apart) now sound right. Windows' player only
    plays one track of a video, for a Medal clip the microphone, which is why the game was so quiet. FastViewer
    now plays these clips' sound itself: game and mic together, in step with the picture. A new button next to
    the speaker (only there for such a video) lets you hear both, only the game or only the mic.
  - Volume up to 200%. The volume slider now goes past 100% (the small mark in the middle); the keys and the
    mouse wheel go there too. Handy for quiet clips. Loud parts are rounded off instead of crackling.
  - Everything you save from such a clip (Save clip, Send to Discord, Crop, compatible copy, copy with captions)
    gets ONE sound track with game and mic mixed, so Discord and every other player play all of it. "Sound" in
    the trim bar has a volume for each (0 to 200%, remembered, and you hear the change at once in "Play
    selection": while trimming you always hear what will be saved, also when the track button was set to only
    the game or only the mic). "Keep the tracks separate" in the same menu saves them the old way.
  - Keep speed. Watching at another speed than 1x (say 1.25x)? Make GIF, Send to Discord and Save clip now
    have "Keep speed (1.25x)" in their menu: ticked, what you save plays at that speed, sound included, with
    the same pitch you hear in FastViewer. It only shows while the speed isn't 1x and is remembered. Save clip
    opens a small menu for it at another speed (Enter still saves right away); at 1x it is one click as before.
    A Discord copy still fits its size. Faster never gets more pictures a second than the original (or 60).
  - "Make a playable copy" for videos and songs whose sound is missing its description (seen in videos saved
    from VK: they play in Windows' Photos app but not in FastViewer, Movies & TV or many other players). The
    message now says exactly that, and the button makes a copy next to the original with the missing piece
    filled in. Nothing is converted: picture and sound are the very same bytes, so it takes a moment and loses
    no quality. Such files usually don't say how long they are either; the copy does, so its time bar works.
  - Speed changes without a gap. At any speed other than 1x FastViewer plays the sound itself for every
    video, so going from one speed to another no longer drops the sound for half a second: it just gets
    faster or slower. Back at 1x, Windows' player takes the sound over again after two seconds. (For a
    moment after a change the sound can be a fraction of a second ahead of the picture, because Windows'
    player stands still briefly; it settles by itself.)
  - Repeat without a gap: FastViewer's own sound (Medal clips, other speeds, over 100%) goes on from the end
    straight into the start, together with the picture, instead of catching up or starting late. It never
    bends the pitch to stay in step (at most 0.4%, which can't be heard); a larger difference is closed with
    a 10 ms crossfade. At other speeds Windows' player stands still for a moment after starting over: the
    sound then steps back into place once, in a few milliseconds.
  Fixed
  - Changing the speed quickly (dragging the speed slider, holding a key) could freeze the video on one frame
    for good, with later speeds ignored: Windows' player locks up when its speed is set many times in a row.
    A single change now goes to it at once; while you drag, the video simply plays on and takes the speed
    you end on in one step, about a third of a second after you stop. Should a picture ever stand still for
    two seconds while it is meant to play, FastViewer gets it going again, without ever moving the video to
    another place. A video that gets stuck just before its end is taken as ended, so Repeat goes on.
  - The sound can no longer stay away. If FastViewer's own sound fails for any reason it starts over by
    itself; if it stops answering it is replaced; and while it is away Windows' player sounds instead.
    Closing with the X (FastViewer stays ready in the background) now also ends everything about the sound,
    so the next file always starts fresh.
  - A file Windows' player silently gave up on (the VK videos above) could leave the previous file on screen
    for good. FastViewer now notices and says what is wrong with it.
  - Clips cut in Medal's own editor: their second and third sound track were saved almost half a second late.
  Good to know
  - Videos with one sound track, at 1x and 100% or less, play exactly as before, and switching between files
    is as fast as in 1.25.0 (measured back to back, also in a folder of Medal clips).
  - At other speeds than 1x the sound changes pitch (higher when faster), for every video.
  - Which track is the game and which the mic is taken from the order Medal uses. FastViewer works out by
    listening that a clip's first track is Medal's own mix of the others and leaves that one out.


1.25.0  (11 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Captions burned into the video, for Discord (which doesn't show subtitle files). In Send to Discord (trim
    bar) tick "Burn in captions": it appears whenever the video has captions or subtitles. Or use video "..." >
    Subtitles > Save a copy with captions for a normal copy. The text looks like FastViewer's own subtitles
    (white on a dark box, your subtitle size, any language), every sound track is kept, and nothing extra has
    to be installed.
  - Better quality captions: an optional larger Whisper model (547 MB, one button in Settings > Videos, size
    and progress shown, works offline). Once it's there it is used instead of the small one; Remove goes back.
    On nine test songs with known words it got 25% of the words wrong where the small model gets 30%. It is
    slower: about a minute for each minute of video on a mid-range processor (the small one: a quarter of that).
  Changed
  - Automatic captions understand songs much better, with the small model too: 30% of the words wrong on the
    test songs instead of 42%. Whisper used to answer "[music]" for half a minute of singing at a time; now it
    writes the words it hears. A sung line that repeats is no longer dropped, a line can no longer get stuck
    repeating to the end of a song, and made-up lines (credits like "subtitles by ...", which Whisper invents
    when it can't make out any words) are left out. Captions made before stay as they were: video "..." >
    Subtitles > Make the captions again.
  - A tidier video "..." menu (13 rows instead of up to 19). Speed is one row with a slider that stops at
    0.25x to 5x and shows the speed; click the speed to go back to 1x (the mouse wheel over the row works too).
    Everything about subtitles sits under one "Subtitles" item that opens a small menu beside it; that menu
    stays open while you move the pointer to it, also across other rows. "Make smoother" is one row with a
    2x and a 4x button. The S key and all other keys work as before.
  - "Speeds in the menu" (Settings > Videos) is gone: the slider has every speed. The Slower and Faster keys
    now step through all of them.
  - Settings > Buttons > "..." menus lists the same, shorter video menu.


1.24.0  (11 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Subtitles. A subtitle file (.srt or .vtt) with the same name as the video, next to it, shows by itself (also
    "video.en.srt"). A CC button appears on the video bar for such videos: click it, or press S, to switch the
    subtitles off and on. Video "..." > Choose subtitles picks any other file. The text sits over the bottom of
    the video and glides up out of the way when the control bar shows. Size: Settings > Videos > Subtitle size.
  - Automatic captions: FastViewer writes the subtitles itself by listening to the video (video "..." > Make
    captions automatically). It happens on your own PC, nothing is uploaded, and it works without internet. Each
    line appears as soon as it's written, and the captions are remembered for the next time. "Save captions as
    a file" makes a normal .srt. This needs a one-time download of 190 MB (Whisper): one button in Settings >
    Videos > Automatic captions, with the size on the button and a progress line. Many languages; if it guesses
    the language wrong, pick it under "Language spoken in your videos".
  - Make smoother: doubles or quadruples a video's frames (30 becomes 60 or 120 a second) on your graphics card
    and saves the result as a new video next to the original (video "..." > Make smoother). A one-time download
    of 47 MB (Settings > Videos > Make videos smoother). FastViewer looks at your graphics card first and says
    plainly whether it's fine, will be slow, or is too weak. Phone videos filmed in HDR come out in normal
    colours. Needs a few GB of free disk space while it works; FastViewer checks that before it starts.
  - Convert to JPG / Convert to PNG in the top "..." menu, for any photo FastViewer can open (WebP, HEIC, AVIF,
    JFIF, BMP...). The copy appears next to the original, upright as shown; the original is never touched and no
    file is ever replaced. See-through parts become white in a JPG and stay see-through in a PNG.
  - Both downloads come straight from their makers' pages (GitHub and Hugging Face), only the files needed are
    fetched, and each one is checked against a fingerprint built into FastViewer before it's used. "Remove" in
    the same place takes them off your PC again.
  - Rotate left: Ctrl+Shift+R, "..." > Rotate left, or right-click the rotate button, turns a photo or video
    counter-clockwise, with the same animation and the same saving into the file as the normal rotate. Settings >
    Viewing > "The rotate button turns" picks which way a normal click goes (right-click is always the other way;
    the arrow on the button shows it).
  Changed
  - "Rotate" is now called "Rotate right" in the menu and on the Keyboard page.
  - All new menu items can be switched off or moved in Settings > Buttons > "..." menus, the CC button in
    Settings > Buttons, and S is a key like any other in Settings > Keyboard.
  Fixed
  - Cropping a see-through WebP picture no longer turns its see-through parts black.


1.23.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  A polish release: no new features, everything that was there looks and moves better.
  Changed
  - The File info pane slides in and out, and the picture glides to its new place instead of jumping. Before, the
    pane just appeared and had no closing animation at all.
  - Animations no longer skip their first part when the PC is busy for a moment (opening Settings, changing pages,
    full screen, changing looks): each one starts from its beginning.
  - New side arrows: larger, with a clear chevron that leans the way it goes when you point at it. In Midnight
    they no longer take on the colour of the video behind them.
  - Nothing open, an empty folder or a file that can't be opened now shows an icon, a headline, the reason and a
    proper button ("Open a file" / "Open with another app") instead of a few lines of grey text.
  - Settings is a screen of its own: the title bar says "Settings" and the greyed-out file buttons step aside.
    In Liquid Glass the photo no longer shows through the pages.
  - Settings is calmer to read: long explanations show their first sentence with "More" behind it. Point at More
    for the rest, or click it to open the whole text in the card. Nothing was removed.
  - Appearance: the look previews have rounded corners and share the row evenly (all five side by side when
    there's room).
  - Keyboard: an empty key box shows a quiet "+" instead of "Not set" thirty times, and key boxes have an
    outline in every look, so they read as boxes you can click.
  - With no file open, the zoom buttons and slider are off like the other file buttons.
  - Icons in the menus are the same size and weight as the ones in the bars.
  - Delete dialog and messages: Cancel, "Start over", "Update now" and the like are outlined buttons in every
    look (in Clean and Midnight they were bare text), and messages carry a small icon.
  - Grid: videos and songs show their length on the thumbnail, and a search that finds nothing says so in the
    middle of the page.
  - Mini player and very small windows: smaller side arrows and middle play button, and the control bar keeps
    clear of the arrows.
  - The seconds inside the skip buttons are a little larger.
  - "Couldn't save" is an in-app message now, not a Windows box.
  - Memory: while FastViewer is minimized (5 seconds) or another app has been in front for 30 seconds, it closes
    the videos it keeps ready out of sight (about 30 MB each in this version's measurements, more for large
    videos). The file on screen is never touched, nothing is closed while something plays, and they are loaded
    again the moment you come back (the window becomes active or the pointer moves onto it), so the first
    switch is instant as always.
  Fixed
  - Opening a file no longer builds the "drop a photo here" screen first.


1.22.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Make GIF: in the trim bar (Ctrl+T), choose the part with the handles and press "Make GIF": Small (320 pixels),
    Medium (480) or Large (640), up to a minute long. FastViewer writes the GIF itself: nothing else to install.
  - Send to Discord: also in the trim bar. Makes an .mp4 of the part between the handles that fits under 20 MB
    (Discord without Nitro), 50 MB or 500 MB and copies the file: paste it into a chat with Ctrl+V. The picture
    only gets smaller or loses frames when it has to; a video that's already small enough is copied as it is.
    The copies live in a temporary folder and are cleared after a week.
  - Crop photos and videos: press C (or "..." > Crop). Drag the frame's corners or sides, drag inside it to move
    it, or drag outside it for a new one; Shape keeps it Original, Square, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3 or 3:4. "Save a copy"
    leaves the original alone. "Save" keeps only the cropped part and moves the original to the Recycle Bin, so
    you can always put it back. Photos keep their type; videos are saved as .mp4.
  Changed
  - The Trim button is now a pair of scissors.
  Fixed
  - Videos with more than one sound track (like Medal clips with the game and your microphone apart): Save clip
    and Make compatible copy kept only one of them, and not always the first. Every way of saving a video (Save
    clip, compatible copy, Send to Discord, Crop) now keeps all sound tracks, in the same order as the original.


1.21.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Search the grid: type in the search box at the top of "All files" (or press Ctrl+F there) to see only files
