FastViewer - what's new
=======================

Version numbers: major.minor.patch. New features raise the middle number, bug fixes the last one.
You can see which version you have in "..." -> Settings -> About.


1.25.0  (11 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Captions burned into the video, for Discord (which doesn't show subtitle files). In Send to Discord (trim
    bar) tick "Burn in captions": it appears whenever the video has captions or subtitles. Or use video "..." >
    Subtitles > Save a copy with captions for a normal copy. The text looks like FastViewer's own subtitles
    (white on a dark box, your subtitle size, any language), every sound track is kept, and nothing extra has
    to be installed.
  - Better quality captions: an optional larger Whisper model (547 MB, one button in Settings > Videos, size
    and progress shown, works offline). Once it's there it is used instead of the small one; Remove goes back.
    On nine test songs with known words it got 25% of the words wrong where the small model gets 30%. It is
    slower: about a minute for each minute of video on a mid-range processor (the small one: a quarter of that).
  Changed
  - Automatic captions understand songs much better, with the small model too: 30% of the words wrong on the
    test songs instead of 42%. Whisper used to answer "[music]" for half a minute of singing at a time; now it
    writes the words it hears. A sung line that repeats is no longer dropped, a line can no longer get stuck
    repeating to the end of a song, and made-up lines (credits like "subtitles by ...", which Whisper invents
    when it can't make out any words) are left out. Captions made before stay as they were: video "..." >
    Subtitles > Make the captions again.
  - A tidier video "..." menu (13 rows instead of up to 19). Speed is one row with a slider that stops at
    0.25x to 5x and shows the speed; click the speed to go back to 1x (the mouse wheel over the row works too).
    Everything about subtitles sits under one "Subtitles" item that opens a small menu beside it; that menu
    stays open while you move the pointer to it, also across other rows. "Make smoother" is one row with a
    2x and a 4x button. The S key and all other keys work as before.
  - "Speeds in the menu" (Settings > Videos) is gone: the slider has every speed. The Slower and Faster keys
    now step through all of them.
  - Settings > Buttons > "..." menus lists the same, shorter video menu.


1.24.0  (11 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Subtitles. A subtitle file (.srt or .vtt) with the same name as the video, next to it, shows by itself (also
    "video.en.srt"). A CC button appears on the video bar for such videos: click it, or press S, to switch the
    subtitles off and on. Video "..." > Choose subtitles picks any other file. The text sits over the bottom of
    the video and glides up out of the way when the control bar shows. Size: Settings > Videos > Subtitle size.
  - Automatic captions: FastViewer writes the subtitles itself by listening to the video (video "..." > Make
    captions automatically). It happens on your own PC, nothing is uploaded, and it works without internet. Each
    line appears as soon as it's written, and the captions are remembered for the next time. "Save captions as
    a file" makes a normal .srt. This needs a one-time download of 190 MB (Whisper): one button in Settings >
    Videos > Automatic captions, with the size on the button and a progress line. Many languages; if it guesses
    the language wrong, pick it under "Language spoken in your videos".
  - Make smoother: doubles or quadruples a video's frames (30 becomes 60 or 120 a second) on your graphics card
    and saves the result as a new video next to the original (video "..." > Make smoother). A one-time download
    of 47 MB (Settings > Videos > Make videos smoother). FastViewer looks at your graphics card first and says
    plainly whether it's fine, will be slow, or is too weak. Phone videos filmed in HDR come out in normal
    colours. Needs a few GB of free disk space while it works; FastViewer checks that before it starts.
  - Convert to JPG / Convert to PNG in the top "..." menu, for any photo FastViewer can open (WebP, HEIC, AVIF,
    JFIF, BMP...). The copy appears next to the original, upright as shown; the original is never touched and no
    file is ever replaced. See-through parts become white in a JPG and stay see-through in a PNG.
  - Both downloads come straight from their makers' pages (GitHub and Hugging Face), only the files needed are
    fetched, and each one is checked against a fingerprint built into FastViewer before it's used. "Remove" in
    the same place takes them off your PC again.
  - Rotate left: Ctrl+Shift+R, "..." > Rotate left, or right-click the rotate button, turns a photo or video
    counter-clockwise, with the same animation and the same saving into the file as the normal rotate. Settings >
    Viewing > "The rotate button turns" picks which way a normal click goes (right-click is always the other way;
    the arrow on the button shows it).
  Changed
  - "Rotate" is now called "Rotate right" in the menu and on the Keyboard page.
  - All new menu items can be switched off or moved in Settings > Buttons > "..." menus, the CC button in
    Settings > Buttons, and S is a key like any other in Settings > Keyboard.
  Fixed
  - Cropping a see-through WebP picture no longer turns its see-through parts black.


1.23.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  A polish release: no new features, everything that was there looks and moves better.
  Changed
  - The File info pane slides in and out, and the picture glides to its new place instead of jumping. Before, the
    pane just appeared and had no closing animation at all.
  - Animations no longer skip their first part when the PC is busy for a moment (opening Settings, changing pages,
    full screen, changing looks): each one starts from its beginning.
  - New side arrows: larger, with a clear chevron that leans the way it goes when you point at it. In Midnight
    they no longer take on the colour of the video behind them.
  - Nothing open, an empty folder or a file that can't be opened now shows an icon, a headline, the reason and a
    proper button ("Open a file" / "Open with another app") instead of a few lines of grey text.
  - Settings is a screen of its own: the title bar says "Settings" and the greyed-out file buttons step aside.
    In Liquid Glass the photo no longer shows through the pages.
  - Settings is calmer to read: long explanations show their first sentence with "More" behind it. Point at More
    for the rest, or click it to open the whole text in the card. Nothing was removed.
  - Appearance: the look previews have rounded corners and share the row evenly (all five side by side when
    there's room).
  - Keyboard: an empty key box shows a quiet "+" instead of "Not set" thirty times, and key boxes have an
    outline in every look, so they read as boxes you can click.
  - With no file open, the zoom buttons and slider are off like the other file buttons.
  - Icons in the menus are the same size and weight as the ones in the bars.
  - Delete dialog and messages: Cancel, "Start over", "Update now" and the like are outlined buttons in every
    look (in Clean and Midnight they were bare text), and messages carry a small icon.
  - Grid: videos and songs show their length on the thumbnail, and a search that finds nothing says so in the
    middle of the page.
  - Mini player and very small windows: smaller side arrows and middle play button, and the control bar keeps
    clear of the arrows.
  - The seconds inside the skip buttons are a little larger.
  - "Couldn't save" is an in-app message now, not a Windows box.
  - Memory: while FastViewer is minimized (5 seconds) or another app has been in front for 30 seconds, it closes
    the videos it keeps ready out of sight (about 30 MB each in this version's measurements, more for large
    videos). The file on screen is never touched, nothing is closed while something plays, and they are loaded
    again the moment you come back (the window becomes active or the pointer moves onto it), so the first
    switch is instant as always.
  Fixed
  - Opening a file no longer builds the "drop a photo here" screen first.


1.22.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Make GIF: in the trim bar (Ctrl+T), choose the part with the handles and press "Make GIF": Small (320 pixels),
    Medium (480) or Large (640), up to a minute long. FastViewer writes the GIF itself: nothing else to install.
  - Send to Discord: also in the trim bar. Makes an .mp4 of the part between the handles that fits under 20 MB
    (Discord without Nitro), 50 MB or 500 MB and copies the file: paste it into a chat with Ctrl+V. The picture
    only gets smaller or loses frames when it has to; a video that's already small enough is copied as it is.
    The copies live in a temporary folder and are cleared after a week.
  - Crop photos and videos: press C (or "..." > Crop). Drag the frame's corners or sides, drag inside it to move
    it, or drag outside it for a new one; Shape keeps it Original, Square, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3 or 3:4. "Save a copy"
    leaves the original alone. "Save" keeps only the cropped part and moves the original to the Recycle Bin, so
    you can always put it back. Photos keep their type; videos are saved as .mp4.
  Changed
  - The Trim button is now a pair of scissors.
  Fixed
  - Videos with more than one sound track (like Medal clips with the game and your microphone apart): Save clip
    and Make compatible copy kept only one of them, and not always the first. Every way of saving a video (Save
    clip, compatible copy, Send to Discord, Crop) now keeps all sound tracks, in the same order as the original.


1.21.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Search the grid: type in the search box at the top of "All files" (or press Ctrl+F there) to see only files
    whose name contains what you type. Several words: all must be in the name. Esc clears the search.
  - Sort the grid: Folder order, Name, Newest/Oldest first, Largest/Smallest first, Longest/Shortest first
    (videos and music by length; photos come after them). Only the grid is sorted: the viewer keeps the folder's order.
  - Choose your menus: Settings > Buttons > "..." menus. Switch items of the top "..." menu and the video "..."
    menu on or off and move them up or down. Switched-off items keep their keyboard shortcuts. Your menus are not
    part of a look, so sharing a look never changes someone else's menus.
  - Rotating a video with "Also save it to the file" (Settings > Viewing, now "When you rotate a photo or video")
    turns MP4 and MOV videos for good: the turn is written into the file the way phones do it, so File Explorer's
    thumbnail turns and it plays upright in other players too. Nothing is re-encoded: the picture and sound stay
    exactly the same. Like Change cover, a new copy is written and checked first and only then swapped in. The
    video keeps its place when sorted by date, and keeps playing where it was. Also "..." > Save rotation to file.
  Changed
  - Menu items that shared an icon now each have their own: Copy frame (a picture), Make compatible copy
    (convert arrows), Loop from here (start mark), Loop until here (end mark), Change cover (a music note).
  Fixed
  - Switches that are On could show as a black pill with no knob when the accent colour is dark (for example
    a dark accent in a Custom look): the knob now turns white or black, whichever shows, and the switch gets an
    outline when it would blend into the card behind it.
  - Turning a video (or a photo, without saving it to the file) is now remembered for good. Before, the turn was
    forgotten after an update or when FastViewer fully closed.

1.20.0  (10 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Music: FastViewer now plays .mp3, .m4a, .aac, .wav, .flac and .wma files. You see the album cover (or a
    music note) with the song's title and artist, and the normal video controls: play/pause, the time bar, skip,
    volume, speed, Repeat and A-B loop. Music shows up instantly while you click through a folder, never waiting.
    Long recordings (10 minutes or more, like podcasts) continue where you left off; songs always start at the
    beginning. "Open with" in File Explorer now offers FastViewer for music files too (after Settings > Default app).
  - Change cover (MP3): right-click the music view, or music "..." menu > Change cover..., and pick any picture.
    It is saved into the song as its cover and shows right away, also in File Explorer. The song keeps its place
    when sorted by date. Only the tag at the start of the file changes: the sound stays exactly the same.
    FastViewer writes a new copy first and only swaps it in when that copy checks out, so if anything goes wrong
    (for example another app has the song open) the original stays as it was. No extra programs needed.
  - Taskbar buttons: point at FastViewer's taskbar icon and the preview has Previous, Play/Pause and Next buttons,
    like media players. Play/Pause shows for videos and music and switches icon as you play or pause.
  - Copy frame: copies the video frame on screen to the clipboard, ready to paste into Discord, Paint, Word...
    In the video "..." menu, or press Ctrl+Shift+C. The video keeps playing.
  - Speeds from 0.25x to 5x: 0.25x, 0.5x, 0.75x, 1x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2x, 2.5x, 3x, 4x and 5x. Pick the ones
    you want in the video "..." menu in Settings > Videos > "Speeds in the menu" (0.5x, 1x, 1.5x and 2x until you
    change it). The Slower/Faster keys step through the same ones.
  Changed
  - Settings > Viewing > Show now has: Everything, Photos and videos, Only photos, Only videos, Only music.
    Your earlier choice is kept ("Only videos" still means just videos).
  Fixed
  - Rotating a photo with "Also save it to the file" now really shows everywhere: the turn was saved, but File
    Explorer (and FastViewer's thumbnail strip and grid) kept showing the old, unturned thumbnail, because the
    file's date was put back exactly as it was. The date now moves by a hundredth of a second (the photo keeps its
    place when sorted by date), Windows is made to redraw its saved thumbnails of the photo in every size, and
    File Explorer is told to show them. Works for JPG (also phone photos), PNG, BMP and TIFF.

1.19.0  (9 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Mini player: photos with see-through parts (PNG, GIF, ...) now really are see-through. There is no black
    or grey box behind the picture any more, so the mini player only covers what's actually in the picture.
    Photos and videos without see-through parts look the same as before.
  - Liquid Glass: every button now squishes when you press it and springs back with a little wobble, like on
    an iPhone: also the side arrows, switches, the Settings side menu and every button on every Settings page.
    Even a very quick click plays the whole squish. A Custom look based on Liquid Glass does it too.
  - Skip buttons have a clearer icon in every look: an open circle with an arrow pointing back or forward and
    a bigger number inside (it used to look like a "reload" sign).
  - Custom look > Motion: the styles now feel really different. Smooth is like Windows 11, Subtle is quick and
    barely moves, Springy travels far and bounces, Calm fades slowly in and out, and the new Snappy gets there
    almost at once. Speed now goes from Fastest (0.4x) through Extra fast, Fast, Normal, Slow and Extra slow to
    Super slow (3x). Your old "Faster" and "Slower" choices become Fast and Slow. The speed now applies to
    everything that opens or closes (menus, messages, dialogs, Settings, the colour picker...), while zooming,
    panning and wheel scrolling keep following your hand at their usual pace.
  - Menus now also fade out when you close them by clicking elsewhere, clicking their button again or pressing
    Esc, at the look's speed (before, only choosing an item faded them).
  Fixed
  - Liquid Glass: faint shapes of the next and previous files could show beside the picture. The files that
    are loaded ahead are now completely hidden, and switching is just as instant as before.
  - Thin blue lines around the mini player and along the screen edges in full screen. They were Windows' own
    window border, drawn in your accent colour when "Show accent colour on title bars and window borders" is
    on. Full screen and the mini player now have no Windows border at all.
1.18.0  (2 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Settings > Buttons > "Tips when pointing at a button": switch off the little notes that appear when you
    point at a button. Screen readers still read every button's name, and the keys stay listed in the "..."
    menus and Settings > Keyboard.
1.17.2  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed
  - Scrolling very fast through videos could lag a little. Two causes: the protection for slow PCs (which
    stops taking video snapshots) switched on after a single short hiccup, for example while a game or a
    call kept the PC busy, and then every video switch waited for the video to play. It now only switches
    on when snapshots are really slow (one over 0.4 s, or three slow ones within 30 s). And the time bar's
    dot no longer redraws the window while you click through files.
  - Clicking a menu's button (or anywhere else) to close the menu flickered: the menu now closes at once,
    like Windows' own menus. Choosing an item still fades it out.
  - Keys like ` showed as "Oem3" in menus and Settings: they now show the character on your keyboard.
  - Mini player: scrolling on the volume button could switch to another video, because the volume message
    appeared under the mouse. The volume now shows as a number next to the speaker, and scrolling on the
    mini player's buttons never changes the file.
1.17.1  (2 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Make your own look: Settings > Custom look. Start from any look and change what you like, in plain groups:
    the main colours (accent, background, bars, pages, cards and menus, text), the font and page titles,
    corner roundness, docked or floating bars, a see-through window, Windows or line icons, and the motion
    style and speed. "All colours" lets you set every one of the over 50 parts on its own. Dark and light
    each get their own colours.
  - Every change shows on the whole window right away. The colour picker has a shade square, hue,
    see-through, the colour code, ready-made colours and "Original" to go back.
  - FastViewer warns when text becomes hard to read with your colours, and text on the accent colour
    picks black or white by itself.
  - Share looks: Export saves a small .fvlook file; Import (or dropping the file on FastViewer) uses it
    straight away, with Undo. "Start over" goes back to a plain copy of the look you started from.
  - Your look appears as a fifth card on Settings > Appearance, with its own name.
  Fixed
  - The pause sign in the big button in the middle of a video sat a little left of centre (most visible in
    Liquid Glass and Midnight). Play and pause are both centred again.
1.16.1  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed
  - Clicking "..." while its menu was open closed the menu and opened it again right away. Now clicking a
    menu's button again closes it, like everywhere in Windows: the "..." menus, the zoom % menu, the
    thumbnail size menu in the grid and the drop-down lists in Settings.
  - Pressing a key again now undoes it everywhere: Ctrl+T leaves trimming and Ctrl+, closes Settings (as G
    already closed the grid and I the File info).
  - Switching to the mini player while trimming ends trimming instead of squeezing the trim bar into the
    small window.
  - Dragging the time bar quickly while a video played made it stutter, and its sound could keep stuttering
    long after pausing. While you drag, the video now holds still and shows the frames you pass, then plays on
    from where you let go.
  Improved
  - The dot on the time bar glides with the video at your screen's refresh rate instead of stepping along.
1.16.0  (2 Nov 2026)
  New
  - Smooth animations everywhere: buttons, switches, menus, Settings, messages, the Delete dialog, the
    controls fading in, play/pause, the volume slider, zooming (wheel steps glide into each other),
    panning (with momentum), rotating, the grid (the photo shrinks into its tile and grows back out),
    thumbnails, the strip, the Info pane, full screen, the mini player and changing looks. They run at your
    screen's refresh rate, also 144, 240 or 500 Hz (with several screens: the fastest), take just as long at
    any rate, and stop completely when nothing moves. Settings > About > Screen shows your refresh rate and how
    many frames a second the animations really got.
  - Each look moves in its own way: Photos like Windows 11, Clean subtle, Liquid Glass springy (buttons
    press in), Midnight calm.
  - Settings > Appearance > Animations: Like Windows (follows Windows' "Show animations"), Full, Reduced
    (short fades only) or Off.
  - Switching between files is still instant. If you like, Settings > Appearance > "Transition between
    files" > Crossfade softly fades the old file out (off by default).
  - Trim is new: a filmstrip of the video with a handle at each end. Drag a handle and the video shows
    that exact frame; the times sit under the handles and the clip length at the top. "Play selection"
    plays just that part, then Save clip. Keys: left/right move a handle by one frame (Shift: a second),
    Tab picks the other handle, [ and ] put a handle where the video is, Space plays, Enter saves, Esc
    leaves.
  - Mini player: an optional volume button (Settings > Appearance, off by default). Click to mute, scroll
    to change the volume, point at it for the slider.
  Improved
  - Liquid Glass: the blue of buttons and highlights is a little darker, so white text on it is easier to
    read.
  - "Keep on top" says whether it's on or off.
  Fixed
  - Photos look (1.15.6): its name was missing in Settings and Light mode showed dark colours.
  - File Explorer could show a FastViewer badge on every photo and video thumbnail after FastViewer was
    added to "Open with" by an older version. FastViewer now removes it by itself (Windows may keep old
    thumbnails until it remakes them: Disk Cleanup > Thumbnails clears them at once).
1.15.6  (2 Nov 2026)
  Improved (a look at every screen, look and window size)
  - Keyboard and screen readers: Settings can now be used with the keyboard alone (Tab, Space or Enter,
    arrow keys in menus, Esc to go back), with a clear focus ring. Every button has a name that screen
    readers read out. In a shortcut box, Delete removes the key and Tab moves on.
  - Tooltips and menus always show the keys you set: after changing a shortcut they used to show the old one.
  - Small windows: the time bar stays (the skip and repeat buttons make room instead), the play button in the
    middle gets smaller, the Delete message fits, the Info button no longer sits on the zoom box, and the
    Settings side menu scrolls so every page can be reached.
  - Clean look: the File info pane is no longer covered by the floating bars.
  - Files that can't be opened get a plain explanation ("This file is empty", "This video is incomplete,
    for example a download that didn't finish"...) and an "Open with another app" button. A video that
    failed while you switched very fast gets another try when you go back to it.
  - An empty window no longer shows the last file's details, arrows and file buttons.
  - Easier to read: darker hint text in the light looks and stronger backgrounds behind the side arrows,
    so they show on bright photos too.
  - While Settings is open, the file buttons (Delete, Copy, Rotate, Open with) are off, as in the grid.
  - Slideshow: the button turns into "Stop slideshow", a short message says how to stop it, and full screen
    shows only the Close button in the corner.
  - The first time FastViewer stays ready in the background after you close it, a notice by the clock
    says so (and how to quit). Preload changes right away instead of after a restart.
  - Trim shows long clips as "(20:00 long)" instead of seconds. File info dates are in English.
  - Settings > Keyboard has an icon for every action. A shortened file name in the title bar shows in full
    when you point at it.
  - A grey Windows accent colour made buttons and switches look switched off: FastViewer uses blue then.
  Fixed
  - Changing Preload in one FastViewer window could make another open window crash on its next switch.
1.15.5  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed
  - "Couldn't check for updates: The remote server returned an error: (403) Forbidden." GitHub answers
    only 60 update questions an hour per internet connection. When it says no, FastViewer now asks
    GitHub's normal releases page instead (which has no such limit), and if that fails too it says
    "GitHub is busy right now. FastViewer will try again later." and quietly tries again later.
    Updates still only ever download from FastViewer's own GitHub releases.
  - In full screen, Settings' Back button and title sat under the top bar's buttons. The top bar now
    stays above Settings, like in the window.
1.15.4  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed (found in a big stress test: thousands of fast switches, odd files, broken settings)
  - A video you skipped past just as it was starting (next, then quickly back) kept playing out of
    sight, with sound, and was already over when you went to it.
  - The Delete message named one file, but if the mouse wheel, a mouse side button or the slideshow
    moved on while it was open, OK deleted the file shown by then. Now it always deletes the file it
    names, and nothing moves on while it's open.
  - A damaged or hand-edited settings.ini could stop FastViewer from starting at all, or quietly reset
    every setting after one unreadable line. Unreadable lines are now skipped (and noted in
    %TEMP%\FastViewer\crash.txt) and odd values are kept within range.
  - Leaving Settings while a shortcut box said "Press a key..." made the next key you pressed
    anywhere become that shortcut.
  - With "Also save it to the file" on, a photo you rotated and then quickly left wasn't saved.
  - Updating with a second FastViewer window open (or one waiting in the background) failed with
    "Windows doesn't allow changing files". Updating now works, and the FastViewer waiting in the
    background makes way for the new version.
  - Folders with thousands of files sorted by date (or with shuffle on) froze FastViewer for seconds,
    also each time a file was added. Now well under half a second.
  - Animated GIFs used far too much memory (over 2 GB for a folder of HD GIFs): only the GIF on
    screen keeps its frames now.
  - Closing the window while "Saving clip" or "Make compatible copy" was still busy left a broken,
    half-written video. The window now disappears and FastViewer closes once the video is saved.
  - Where you left off in videos: several FastViewer windows wiped out each other's places, and the
    list of 300 forgot videos you had just watched again.
1.15.3  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed
  - The mouse wheel over the speaker button changes the volume again (it stopped in 1.15.0, when
    pointing at the speaker started opening the volume slider).

1.15.2  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed
  - In the Clean look and in full screen, the message at the top ("Update now", "Start over",

1.15.1  (2 Nov 2026)
  Fixed
  - With more than one FastViewer window open (or one waiting in the background), a setting changed
    in one window didn't reach the others: they kept the old one (for example the video still
    dimmed with "Dim video" off) and could even put it back when they closed. Now every window
    picks up changes made in another as soon as you switch to it.
  - If FastViewer ever can't save its settings, the reason goes to %TEMP%\FastViewer\crash.txt.

1.15.0  (1 Nov 2026)
  Changed
  - Volume: point at the speaker button and the volume slider opens by itself; it closes a moment
    after you move away. Clicking the speaker now mutes and unmutes (like YouTube). On a touch
    screen a tap opens the slider.

1.14.0  (1 Nov 2026)

      Photos        like the Windows 11 Photos app (how FastViewer always looked)

1.13.0  (1 Nov 2026)
  Better
  - Uses much less memory with big photos: they're kept at screen size (a 12 MP photo takes
    about 10 MB instead of 48 MB), and the full photo loads by itself when you zoom in further.
    Going through a folder of 12 MP photos now peaks around 250 MB instead of 1.3 GB.
  - Pictures FastViewer no longer needs are given back to Windows right away, once you pause.
  - Photos open a little faster too (less to decode).
  Fixed
