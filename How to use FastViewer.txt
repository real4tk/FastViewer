FastViewer 1.27.0 - a fast photo, video and music viewer for Windows 10 and 11
======================================================================

No install needed: FastViewer.exe is the whole program.
See "What's new.txt" for what changed in each version.

1. Put FastViewer.exe in a folder where it can stay (for example Documents\FastViewer).
   Don't run it straight from the zip. From now on FastViewer updates itself: it offers
   new versions when it starts (Settings -> Updates). Your settings are kept.

2. The first time you open it, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC".
   That's because it's a home-made app without a paid signature.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. To open your videos with it when you double-click them:
   right-click a video -> Open with -> Choose another app -> More apps ->
   "Look for another app on this PC" -> pick FastViewer.exe -> tick "Always use this app".
   (Or start FastViewer.exe on its own: the welcome screen has "Make FastViewer my default viewer",
   which opens Windows' Default apps; pick FastViewer for Photo viewer and Video player there.)
   Started without a file, FastViewer also shows the files you opened last: click one to open it,
   right-click it to take it off the list ("Clear recent" empties the list).

Mini player
  Ctrl + M (or the button left of "Open with") shows just the video in a small
  window that stays on top of everything. It takes the shape of the video.
  Drag the video to move it, drag the edges to resize, double-click or Esc to go back.
  Photos with see-through parts (PNG, GIF) stay see-through: only the picture covers your screen.

Trimming a video
  Ctrl + T (or video "..." -> Trim) shows the video as a filmstrip with a handle at each end.
  Drag the handles to the part you want to keep: the video shows the frame under the handle.
  "Play selection" plays just that part; "Save clip" saves it as a new video.
  Keys: Left / Right move a handle one frame (Shift: one second), Tab picks the other handle,
  Space plays, Enter saves, Esc cancels.
  "Make GIF" saves that part as an animated GIF. "Send to Discord" makes it fit in 20, 50 or 500 MB
  and copies it: paste it into a chat with Ctrl + V.

Sound: game clips, loudness, files that won't play
  A clip with several sound tracks (Medal: game and microphone apart) plays with all of them together.
  The button next to the speaker (only there for such a clip) picks both, only the game or only the mic.
  The volume goes up to 200%: the mark in the middle of the slider is 100%.
  Settings -> Videos -> "Even out volume" (on) makes loud and quiet files sound about equally loud; your
  volume works on top of it.
  Anything you save from such a clip gets one mixed sound track, so Discord plays all of it. "Sound" in
  the trim bar sets how loud each track is (you hear it at once); "Keep the tracks separate" saves them
  as they were.
  Watching at another speed? "Keep speed" in the Make GIF, Send to Discord and Save clip menus saves it
  at that speed, sound included.
  "Can't open this file" with "Make a playable copy": the file's sound is missing its description (videos
  saved from VK). The button makes a repaired copy next to it, without converting anything.

Cropping a photo or video
  C (or "..." -> Crop) puts a frame over the picture. Drag its corners or sides, or drag inside it to
  move it; Shape keeps it square, 16:9 and so on. "Save a copy" makes a new file; "Save" keeps only
  the cropped part and moves the original to the Recycle Bin. Esc leaves without cropping.

Subtitles and automatic captions
  A subtitle file (.srt or .vtt) with the same name as the video, in the same folder, shows by itself.
  The CC button on the video bar (or the S key) switches subtitles off and on; video "..." -> Subtitles ->
  Choose subtitles picks another file. No subtitle file? "Make captions automatically" there lets
  FastViewer write them by listening, on your own PC. The first time it asks to download 190 MB once
  (also in Settings -> Videos -> Automatic captions); after that it works without internet.
  Songs and videos with music: captions understand singing better than before. For the best result,
  "Better quality captions" in the same place downloads a much larger (slower) Whisper model once
  (547 MB). Made captions before? Video "..." -> Subtitles -> Make the captions again.
  Faster captions: "Faster captions (Parakeet)" in the same place (about 760 MB once) writes captions about 6 times
  faster, for 25 European languages (other languages still use Whisper, by itself). "Caption engine" lets you choose.
  Captions in Discord: Discord doesn't show subtitle files, so the text has to be part of the video.
  In "Send to Discord" tick "Burn in captions" (shown when the video has captions or subtitles), or
  use video "..." -> Subtitles -> Save a copy with captions for a normal copy with the text drawn in.
  Editing subtitles: video "..." -> Subtitles -> Edit subtitles (Ctrl + E). Click a line to go there, type to
  change it, - and + move its start or end (0.1 s), "Now" sets it to this moment, "Shift all" moves every line
  (subtitles a bit early or late), Add line here / Delete / Merge with next, Ctrl + Z undoes. "Save as..."
  makes a new .srt; your original only changes when you click Save and then "Replace ...?".
  English under the subtitles (for learning a language): video "..." -> Subtitles -> Translate to English.
  It starts at the line you're watching; the English shows under the original as each line is done.
  "Show original" / "Show English" in the same menu switch either off. One-time download of about
  1.2 GB (Settings -> Videos -> English translation); a film takes about a minute with a good graphics
  card, about 6 minutes on the processor. Burned-in captions and the subtitle editor show both.

Smoother video
  Video "..." -> Make smoother (2x or 4x the frames) saves a smoother copy next to the original, made
  on your graphics card. One-time download of 47 MB (Settings -> Videos -> Make videos smoother);
  FastViewer tells you first whether your graphics card is up to it.

Convert a photo
  "..." at the top -> Convert to JPG / Convert to PNG makes a copy next to the original (handy for
  .webp pictures from the web). The original stays as it is.

All files in the folder
  G (or the grid button in the bottom bar) shows every photo and video in the folder as
  thumbnails, in File Explorer's order. Click one to open it; arrows + Enter work too. Names shows file names.
  Type in the search box (Ctrl + F) to find files by name; Sort orders them by name, date, size or length.
  Ctrl + mouse wheel makes the thumbnails bigger or smaller. Esc goes back.
  Settings > Viewing > Thumbnail strip adds a band of thumbnails under the picture.

Handy keys
  Left / Right         previous / next file (instant, also for videos)
  Space / click video  play / pause
  J / L                skip back / forward (length set in Settings)
  , / .                one frame back / forward
  [  ]  \              A-B loop: set start, set end, stop
  Ctrl + T             trim: save part of a video as a new file, a GIF or a Discord-sized copy
  C                    crop a photo or video
  S                    subtitles on / off
  Ctrl + E             edit subtitles
  Ctrl + Shift + C     copy the video frame on screen (paste it in Discord, Paint...)
  Up / Down            volume (up to 200%)
  Ctrl + R             rotate 90 degrees (also the rotate button at the top)
  Ctrl + Shift + R     rotate the other way (or right-click the rotate button)
  Ctrl + M             mini player
  G                    all files in the folder (grid)
  F or F11             full screen
  Delete               move to Recycle Bin
  Ctrl + mouse wheel   zoom

Settings are in the "..." menu at the top. Appearance picks a look (Photos, Clean, Liquid Glass,
Midnight; dark or light) and how much moves (Animations); Custom look lets you make your own (colours,
font, shapes, motion) and share it as a .fvlook file; Buttons picks which buttons you see and what the "..." menus list. Every option has a short explanation;
every action's keys can be changed in Settings > Keyboard (two keys each), and the mouse
buttons in Settings > Mouse. Esc always goes back. X shuffles the folder.
Closing FastViewer keeps it ready in the background (icon by the clock) so files open instantly.
While it is minimized or behind another app for a while, it frees the memory of the videos it keeps
ready out of sight, and gets them ready again the moment you come back.
In Settings, "More" behind an explanation shows the rest of it.
Each file you open from File Explorer while FastViewer is open gets a window of its own.
Music (.mp3 .m4a .wav .flac ...) plays too, with its album cover, title and artist.
Point at FastViewer's taskbar icon for Previous / Play-Pause / Next buttons.
The Info pane (the (i) button at the bottom) shows a video's frame rate and video format (H.264, HEVC...).
Something went wrong? Settings -> About -> Copy error report, then paste it into a message to whoever
helps you with FastViewer. It holds no file names, and nothing is sent by itself.
Right-click an MP3's music view to change its cover. Settings > Viewing > Show picks photos, videos and/or music.
Rotating a video or photo is remembered; Settings > Viewing can save the turn into the file (JPEG, MP4, MOV: no quality lost).
Playback speed (0.25x to 5x): the slider at the top of the video "..." menu; click the speed next to it for 1x.
Hold the left mouse button on a video or song to watch at 2x while you hold it; let go for the speed you had.
Videos and songs play in FastViewer's own player: pausing, skipping, speed changes and Repeat take effect at once. Should a file act up, Settings -> Videos -> "FastViewer's own video player" off plays it in Windows' player as before.
Don't want double-click for full screen? Settings -> Viewing -> "Double-click for full screen" (F still works).
Choppy video? Ctrl+Shift+P shows playback stats (frames shown per second, skipped, late).
Other speeds keep the normal pitch; Settings -> Videos -> "Change pitch with speed" makes faster sound higher again.
Videos that don't play or get no thumbnail elsewhere: video "..." -> "Make compatible copy".
iPhone videos (HEVC) need "HEVC Video Extensions" from the Microsoft Store, same as the Photos app.
